## 📅 Election Timeline

### Stage 1: Election of the National Assembly (3 Days)
*Citizens elect Members of Congress (M.C.)*

| Day | Governorates Voting |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Day 1** | Yellow (Auryni-Yeloni 🟡), Red (Korvan 🔴), Cyan (Veyrithi-Marash 💎) |
| **Day 2** | Purple (Ivarra 🟣), Lime (Zahrani 🟩), Green (Sundralic 🟢) |
| **Day 3** | Blue (Valmorri 🔵) |

**Result:** 300 M.C. elected across all 33 states

---

### Stage 2: Election of the Senate (2 Days)
*Members of Congress elect Senators*

| Day | Governorates Holding Senate Elections |
|-----|---------------------------------------|
| **Day 1** | All Governorates EXCEPT Blue (Valmorri 🔵) |
| **Day 2** | Blue Governorate (Valmorri 🔵) only |

**Process:** In each of the 33 states, the newly elected M.C. from that state vote to elect **3 Senators** to represent their state in the Senate.

**Result:** 99 Senators elected (3 per state)

---

### Stage 3: Election of National Cabinet & High Senate (2 Days)

| Day | Process |
|-----|---------|
| **Day 1** | **Election of National Cabinet** – In each state, the 3 Senators from that state elect **1 Minister** to the National Cabinet. This produces 33 Ministers (one per state). |
| **Day 2** | **Election of High Senate** – The 33 Ministers of the National Cabinet vote to elect the 3-member High Senate:<br>• President of the FSA<br>• Vice President of the FSA<br>• Chief Secretary of the FSA |

**Result:** National Cabinet (33) + High Senate (3) = Complete federal leadership

## 🏛️ Powers and Responsibilities

### National Assembly (300 M.C.)

| Authority | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **State Legislation** | Propose and vote on laws affecting their specific state |
| **Senate Elections** | Elect the 3 Senators for their state |
| **Budget Proposals** | Submit state budget requests |
| **Local Oversight** | Monitor state-level implementation of federal laws |

### Senate (99 Senators)

| Authority | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Governorate Legislation** | Propose and vote on laws affecting governorates |
| **Cabinet Elections** | Elect the 33 Ministers of the National Cabinet |
| **Federal Budget Review** | Review and amend federal budget |
| **Confirmation Powers** | Confirm certain federal appointments |
| **Treaty Review** | Review international treaties |

### National Cabinet (33 Ministers)

| Authority | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Executive Functions** | Administer federal ministries and departments |
| **High Senate Elections** | Elect the President, Vice President, and Chief Secretary |
| **Bill Introduction** | Propose federal legislation to the Senate |
| **Budget Formulation** | Prepare the annual federal budget |
| **Policy Implementation** | Execute laws passed by the High Senate |

### High Senate (3 Leaders)

| Position | Authority |
|----------|-----------|
| **President** | State bill approval (without High Senate), veto power, commander-in-chief, foreign relations, emergency powers |
| **Vice President** | State bill approval (joint with President), presidential succession, preside over Senate, tie-breaking vote |
| **Chief Secretary** | Represent Cabinet/Senate/Assembly, introduce government bills, set legislative agenda (cannot introduce personal bills without Cabinet approval) |

---

## 📜 Legislative Process

### Track A: State-Level Bills
1. Bill introduced in National Assembly by M.C.
2. Passed by National Assembly majority
3. Sent to President and Vice President for approval
4. If approved, becomes state law (High Senate approval NOT required)

### Track B: Governorate-Level Bills
1. Bill introduced in Senate by Senator
2. Passed by Senate majority
3. Sent to National Cabinet for review
4. If Cabinet approves, becomes governorate law

### Track C: National-Level Bills
1. Bill introduced by Chief Secretary (with Cabinet approval)
2. Reviewed by National Cabinet
3. Sent to Senate for debate and vote
4. If passed by Senate, sent to High Senate
5. High Senate approves (or vetoes)
6. If vetoed, Senate can override with 2/3 majority
